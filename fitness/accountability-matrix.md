# Accountability Matrix — Road to 10% Body Fat

The contract. Every cycle I grade these six levers **R / A / G** off your Airtable data, give an overall verdict, project your ETA to 10%, and hand you **one** required action. No moralizing — just the numbers against the targets you signed up for.

> **Why these six and nothing else:** they're MECE. Fat loss is driven by energy balance; the other five exist to make sure the loss is *fat* (not muscle), that it's *measured*, and that it doesn't *wreck the race build or your recovery*. Anything not on this list is noise.

---

## The target math (real numbers — logged 2026-07-13)

At **76 kg / 15% BF** wanting **10%**, holding lean mass:
- Lean mass = 76 × (1 − 0.15) = **64.6 kg**
- Target weight = 64.6 ÷ (1 − 0.10) = **71.8 kg**
- **Fat to lose ≈ 4.2 kg** (11.4 kg fat now → 7.2 kg)
- At **0.4 kg/wk** → ~10–11 weeks (~late Sept) · at **0.5 kg/wk** → ~8 weeks (~mid Sept)

**Honest flag — this is the *compatible* path, not a conflict.** You only have ~4.2 kg to lose, which lands you at 10% by **mid-to-late September — before the October peak build and race.** So the plan is clean: run a gentle deficit now, hit 10% by ~mid-Sept, then eat at **maintenance** to fuel the final build and taper. The two goals stop fighting. Caveats: (1) the last 1–2 points to 10% slow down and 10% is genuinely lean; (2) keep the rate ≤0.5 kg/wk so you **lose fat, not watts** — if FTP watts drop as weight falls, you're under-fuelling. Recommended rate: **0.4 kg/wk.**

## The six levers

| # | Lever | What it proves | Metric (from Airtable) | 🟢 Green | 🟠 Amber | 🔴 Red |
|---|-------|----------------|------------------------|---------|---------|-------|
| 1 | **Energy balance** | Fat is actually moving | 7-day avg weight vs required rate | on pace ±0.1 kg/wk & losing | losing but off-pace | flat or gaining |
| 2 | **Protein floor** | Loss is fat, not muscle | avg protein ≥ 2 g/kg lean | ≥ floor, ≥6/7 days | floor 4–5/7 days | < floor most days |
| 3 | **Logging adherence** | You can't manage unmeasured | days with weight **and** kcal | ≥ 6/7 | 4–5/7 | ≤ 3/7 |
| 4 | **Deficit discipline** | Intake matches the plan | avg kcal vs target band | within ±100 | ±100–300 | > 300 over |
| 5 | **Lean-mass stimulus** | Muscle gets a reason to stay | resistance sessions + Z2 volume | 2 lifts + vol target | 1 lift or short vol | 0 lifts |
| 6 | **Recovery guardrail** | Not crashing the engine | loss rate cap + sleep + race conflict | loss ≤1%/wk, sleep ok | loss 1–1.25%/wk | loss >1.25%/wk or banner red |

**Overall grade** = worst of {1,6} caps it (those two are non-negotiable), then weighted by the rest. A Red on lever 1 or 6 can't be averaged away — those are the ones that end cuts badly.

## The weekly verdict format

Each cycle I post a **Weekly Review** row to Airtable and message you:

```
WEEK OF 2026-07-13 · Road to 10%
Overall: 🟠 AMBER — on track but under-fuelled

1 Energy balance  🟢  −0.55 kg/wk (target −0.55) · ETA 10%: 2026-11-24
2 Protein floor   🟠  118 g avg vs 125 floor · 4/7 days
3 Logging         🟢  6/7 days
4 Deficit         🟢  2010 kcal vs 2000 target
5 Lean stimulus   🔴  0 lifts logged — muscle at risk
6 Recovery        🟢  loss 0.7%/wk, sleep 7.1h

ETA to 10%: 2026-11-24 (18 days after target) — SLIPPING
THE ONE THING: book 2 resistance sessions this week. You're
losing weight with zero lifting logged — that's muscle leaving.
```

- **ETA to 10%** is recomputed each week from your real trend, not the plan. If it drifts past your target date, that's flagged **SLIPPING** — the honest early-warning.
- **The one thing** is always a single, concrete, this-week action tied to the worst lever.

## How I run it (the standing review)

I read your **Body Cockpit → Daily Metrics** (weight, BF, kcal, protein, sleep) and **Activities** (sessions, volume) for the trailing 7 days, grade the six levers above, write the verdict row, and notify you. Cadence and delivery are yours to set (see chat). Until sync is live, the review still runs — and a week of empty logs grades **🔴 on adherence**, which is accountability doing its job.

**Escalation ladder** (so "accountability" has teeth):
- 1 Amber week → noted.
- 2 consecutive Reds on the same lever → I open it as a **Red initiative on the Plan** and it leads every review until it clears.
- ETA slips >2 weeks past target → I force the periodization question: cut harder, extend the timeline, or accept post-race finish. Your call, but you have to make it.
